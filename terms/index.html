Using Caption Lanes

Caption Lanes provides assistive live captions and coarse directional hints. Get everyone’s agreement before enabling the microphone.

Follow local consent laws even though this app does not retain raw audio.

Important limitations

Captions and direction estimates can be wrong, delayed, or unavailable. Noise, language support, browsers, and microphone layout affect results.

Caption Lanes does not identify speakers. Do not use it for emergencies, legal records, medical decisions, surveillance, or forensic attribution.

Free and Plus

The free version includes three lanes, caption controls, typed input, and transcript export.

Caption Lanes Plus costs $24 once and adds a fourth lane. A purchase is tied to its license token.
