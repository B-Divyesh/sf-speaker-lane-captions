Plain-language policy

Privacy

Effective 30 August 2026

Caption Lanes supports private, in-person conversations. It does not create accounts, run analytics, sell data, or keep raw audio.

Microphone and captions

After everyone agrees, live audio passes to supported on-device speech tools and a short-lived direction estimator.

Caption Lanes keeps raw audio only in memory while captions are active. It never writes raw audio to storage.

Audio stops when you pause, end, or close the app. The app refuses speech modes that cannot confirm local processing.

Data on your device

Caption text, times, lane choices, display settings, and an optional license stay in your browser. You can export or clear them.

The sample demo uses separate storage. Leaving the demo clears changes made to its sample conversation.

Purchases

Your browser contacts the Sociobot billing API when you buy or verify Caption Lanes Plus. Sociobot/Dodo processes the purchase.

This app stores the returned license locally. It sends the license only for verification.

Network access

The installed app’s files are cached for offline use. Speech also needs an installed on-device language pack.

Caption Lanes contacts another service only for checkout or scheduled license verification.

Your choices

Ask everyone before starting. You can deny microphone access and still use typed captions.

Export or clear saved caption text from Settings. For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
