How it works

How to use Caption Lanes

  1. 1

    Ask the group

    Caption Lanes requests microphone access only after everyone agrees.

  2. 2

    Place the phone

    Set it near the middle. Stereo microphones estimate left, centre, or right.

  3. 3

    Read separate lanes

    Tap a direction when the estimate is uncertain. Export or import caption text in Settings.

Privacy and limits

Know what the app cannot do

Direction is coarse and can be wrong. Caption Lanes does not identify people. Do not use it for emergencies or official records.

  • ✓ Caption text stays in this browser until you clear it.
  • ✓ The app uses no account, analytics, or cloud archive.
  • ✓ Typed captions remain available without microphone access.
